Frequently asked questions

Forgot my password

Can't see a school

Ask an admin to assign it. Your schools are on My profile.

Forecast differs from meals served

Forecasts are estimates and miss one-off events. Plan with the recommended quantity.

Upload marked “Invalid”

Open it to see the problems, fix the file and upload again.
