What you will get

  • Predicted demand — the model's estimate of meals served.
  • Recommended preparation — predicted demand plus a rule-based safety buffer.
  • Meal budget — meals × cost per meal, for day 1 and the whole horizon.
  • A day-by-day table and chart, saved to forecast history.
How this forecast works
  1. The school's recorded history is loaded from the active dataset.
  2. The same features used in training are built for each forecast day.
  3. The saved, already-trained model predicts demand. No model is trained here.
  4. A rule-based safety buffer turns predicted demand into a recommended preparation quantity.
  5. The meal budget multiplies those meal counts by the cost per meal. It never changes the prediction.
  6. The result is saved to the forecast history.

Read results with care

  • Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Multi-day forecasts reuse earlier predictions as if they were observed, so uncertainty grows with the horizon. Treat 30-day forecasts as directional.
  • The safety buffer is a planning heuristic, not a statistical confidence interval.
